Booking and payment

  • One-off cleans: a deposit confirms your date (usually 50% for pre-sale and end of lease cleans, and 30% for carpet steam cleaning). Your quote shows the exact amount. The balance is due on completion.

Cancellations and rescheduling

One-off cleans with a deposit:

  • 48 hours notice or more: full refund of your deposit, or move your date for free.
  • 24 to 48 hours notice: we keep 15% of the quoted total and refund the rest of your deposit.
  • Less than 24 hours notice, or if we can't access the property on the day: we keep 30% of the quoted total and refund the rest of your deposit.

Regular cleans: give us 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule, free of charge. Late cancellations (under 24 hours, or no access on the day) are charged one hour at your rate.

If we ever need to cancel, you get a full refund of anything you've paid.

Our guarantees

  • 100% satisfaction guarantee: not happy with any part of the clean? Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and fix it, free of charge.
  • Bond-back guarantee (end of lease): if your property manager notes a cleaning issue in the exit report, tell us within 72 hours of that inspection and we'll return within 24 hours to fix it, free. This doesn't cover dust or marks that build up if the property stays empty for 7 to 10 days or more after our clean, or that come from tradespeople or inspections.

Access and preparation
